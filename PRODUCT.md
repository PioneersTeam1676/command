# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Members of FRC-style high-school robotics teams (mostly on phones, in a noisy shop, in a hurry), the mentors who run them (leads and admins, at a laptop or tablet reviewing hours and organising events), and parents who follow one student read-only. One deployment is one team.

## Product Purpose

Command is a self-hosted team app: clock in and out, a shared calendar with sign-ups (attend, shifts, things to bring), push reminders, a season leaderboard, a small merch shop, and an admin area for attendance review and exports. Success is that a new student can do the common task (clock in, sign up for a shift, order a hoodie) in one or two taps without being taught, and an admin can run the team without hunting through menus.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, precise, fast. The team's own name, logo and accent colour carry the identity; the interface itself stays out of the way.

## Anti-references

Cluttered admin panels with a dozen tabs. Colourful, decorative SaaS dashboards. Oversized touch-first controls that waste space on desktop. Yellow or saturated accent fills on every button.

## Design Principles

1. One obvious primary action per screen; everything else is quieter.
2. Neutral by default: black and white UI, the team accent only for brand moments, links, focus and live state.
3. Dense but calm: tight type scale, hairline borders, generous whitespace between groups rather than inside them.
4. Works one-handed on a phone and efficiently on a desktop, from the same components.
5. Plain words for labels; no jargon.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast in light and dark, visible focus, 44px minimum touch targets on phones, reduced-motion respected, never colour-only meaning.
