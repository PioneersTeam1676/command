# Design

Geist Sans and Geist Mono. Vercel-style monochrome system: white/near-black surfaces, hairline 1px borders, 6px controls and 8px cards, a neutral primary button (foreground on background), and the team accent reserved for links, focus rings, the brand mark and live indicators. Type: 14px body on desktop and 15px on phones, 24px page titles with tight tracking, monospace for times, counts and prices. Motion: 150ms colour/opacity transitions only.
